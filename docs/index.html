Privacy-first by design

AISynca is designed to work locally in your browser. We do not store your prompts, AI responses, uploaded files, images, account credentials, or session cookies on our servers.

No prompt storage

Your prompts and AI responses are not saved on AISynca servers.

No credential collection

AISynca does not ask for or store your AI account passwords, API keys, or session cookies.

Works with your own accounts

AISynca uses your existing browser sessions for supported AI services.

How it works

Three simple steps to supercharge your multi-AI workflow.

1

Choose AI platforms

Select ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Grok from the AISynca control panel.

2

Write one prompt

Enter your prompt once instead of copying and pasting it across multiple browser tabs.

3

Compare responses

View responses side by side in real time, and choose the best answer for your task.
