Frequently Asked Questions

Why up to four AI platforms simultaneously?

AISynca intentionally supports up to four simultaneous AI platforms to maintain readability on smaller displays and stable performance across a wide range of devices. Four screens offer a balanced layout that maximizes dashboard real estate without layout shifting.

Do I need to pay for each AI platform separately?

AISynca works directly with your existing AI accounts and subscriptions. If you use free plans on Gemini and ChatGPT, you don't pay anything to them. If you subscribe to Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus, AISynca automatically lets you access those paid models and subscriptions directly inside the dashboard, with no extra middleware or API token costs!
