Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

AISynca respects your privacy and is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the AISynca Chrome Extension and related services.

1. Overview

AISynca is a Chrome Extension that allows users to send prompts to multiple AI platforms, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Grok, from a single workspace.

AISynca is designed to work with users' own AI accounts, subscriptions, browser sessions, available models, memories, and platform features. AISynca does not replace those services and does not operate as an API-based AI provider.

2. Information We Collect

AISynca only collects or processes the minimum information necessary to operate the service.

We may collect or process the following information:

  • License and subscription information required to verify paid access and enable Premium features
  • Plan type, license status, subscription validity, and related subscription verification data
  • Support inquiry content voluntarily submitted by users via email
  • Basic information necessary to respond to user requests, bug reports, or feature requests

Contact Forms

AISynca may use Google Forms or similar form services to receive user inquiries, bug reports, feature requests, and support requests.

Information submitted through contact forms is used only to respond to inquiries, provide support, investigate issues, and improve AISynca.

Users should not submit passwords, AI account credentials, session cookies, payment details, confidential documents, or sensitive personal information through contact forms.

Contact form submissions may be processed by the form service provider according to its own terms and privacy policy.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

AISynca does not collect, store, or receive:

  • AI account passwords or login credentials
  • AI service session cookies
  • Chat history from AI platforms
  • Active AI responses
  • Saved memories from AI platforms
  • Prompts typed into the AISynca dashboard
  • Uploaded files, documents, spreadsheets, images, or attachments
  • Browsing history
  • Unrelated website activity
  • Credit card numbers, banking information, or direct payment credentials

4. Browser Extension Permissions

AISynca may request limited browser permissions necessary to provide its core functionality.

These permissions may be used to:

  • Open supported AI service pages
  • Arrange AI service windows or tabs
  • Send user-entered prompts to selected AI platforms
  • Attach files or images when supported
  • Control supported AI interface options, such as model selection or thinking/reasoning settings
  • Maintain user preferences and extension settings

AISynca uses these permissions only to provide user-requested features.

AISynca does not use browser permissions to monitor unrelated browsing activity, collect browsing history, or track users across websites.

5. Prompt, File, and Image Handling

AISynca operates locally within the user's browser.

Prompts, text input, uploaded files, images, and attachments are handled on the user's device and sent directly to the AI platforms selected by the user through the user's browser session.

AISynca does not receive, process, store, or retain your prompts, text input, uploaded files, images, attachments, or AI responses on its own servers.

Your interactions with supported AI platforms are handled through your browser session and are subject to the terms and privacy policies of those platforms.

6. Browsing Activity

AISynca does not collect, store, sell, or share users' browsing history.

AISynca may interact only with supported AI service pages when necessary to provide user-requested features, such as sending prompts, managing layouts, attaching files, or adjusting supported AI settings.

AISynca does not monitor unrelated websites and does not track user activity across the web.

7. Data Sent to AI Platforms

When users choose to send prompts, files, images, or other materials to supported AI platforms, that data is transmitted directly to the selected third-party AI services through the user's browser session.

AISynca does not control how third-party AI services process, store, use, or retain data submitted to them.

Users are responsible for reviewing and complying with the terms and privacy policies of each AI platform they choose to use.

8. Third-Party Services

AISynca may interact with third-party services selected or used by the user, including:

  • ChatGPT by OpenAI
  • Gemini by Google
  • Claude by Anthropic
  • Grok by xAI
  • Lemon Squeezy for payment, licensing, and subscription management

Each third-party service operates under its own terms and privacy policy. We encourage users to review the applicable terms and privacy policies of those services before using them with AISynca.

AISynca is not responsible for the privacy practices, data handling, security, availability, or content processing of third-party services.

9. Payments and Subscriptions

Subscription management, billing, licensing, and payment processing are handled by Lemon Squeezy or another authorized payment and subscription provider.

AISynca may receive limited subscription-related information, such as license status, plan type, and subscription validity, in order to verify access to Premium features.

AISynca does not process or store credit card numbers, banking information, or direct payment credentials.

Payment information is processed by the payment provider according to its own terms and privacy policy.

10. No Sale of Personal Information

AISynca does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information for advertising purposes.

AISynca does not use user data for:

  • Advertising
  • Cross-site tracking
  • Behavioral profiling
  • Selling data to third parties

11. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for the content they choose to submit through AISynca, including prompts, files, images, documents, spreadsheets, and other materials.

Users should not submit sensitive, confidential, proprietary, regulated, or personal information to third-party AI platforms unless they have the right to do so and understand the applicable privacy implications.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Personal information of other individuals
  • Confidential business information
  • Trade secrets
  • Medical, legal, financial, or regulated information
  • Copyrighted or proprietary materials that the user is not authorized to use

12. Data Retention

License and subscription verification records are retained only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide active service access
  • Validate subscriptions
  • Prevent unauthorized use
  • Maintain account and license integrity
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, or fraud prevention obligations

Support inquiry records may be retained as needed to respond to user requests, investigate issues, improve service quality, and maintain support history.

13. Data Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

Users may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that AISynca may hold, such as support inquiry records or subscription-related information.

Some subscription, transaction, tax, or fraud-prevention records may need to be retained by Lemon Squeezy or other third-party providers according to their own legal and operational requirements.

To submit a request, contact us at: AISynca.contact@gmail.com

14. Security

AISynca is designed to minimize the amount of personal information it collects.

Sensitive interactions with AI platforms, including prompts, uploaded files, images, and AI responses, are handled directly through the user's browser and selected third-party AI services.

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. International Data Processing

Third-party services used with AISynca may process information in countries or regions outside the user's country of residence.

By using AISynca with third-party services, users understand that their data may be processed according to the privacy policies, terms, infrastructure, and legal obligations of those third-party providers.

16. Children's Privacy

AISynca is not intended for use by children under the age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

17. Third-Party Platform Changes

AISynca depends on the availability, design, and behavior of supported third-party AI platforms.

Changes to those platforms may affect AISynca's functionality, supported features, data handling flows, or compatibility.

AISynca is not responsible for changes made by third-party AI platforms, including changes to their interfaces, terms, privacy policies, pricing, availability, or supported features.

18. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as AISynca's features, supported AI platforms, legal requirements, or operational practices evolve.

When we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page.

Continued use of AISynca after changes to this Privacy Policy means that users acknowledge the updated policy.

19. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

Email: AISynca.contact@gmail.com
