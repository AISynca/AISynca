Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

AISynca ("the extension") respects user privacy and minimizes data collection.

Information We Collect

AISynca only collects the minimum information required to operate the service.

Collected information:

  • License information required for subscription verification and feature access
  • Inquiry content voluntarily submitted by users

AISynca does not collect:

  • AI account credentials
  • Chat history
  • Saved memories from AI services
  • Prompts
  • Uploaded files
  • Payment information

Payment processing is handled by Lemon Squeezy and payment information is not stored by AISynca.

Prompt and File Handling

AISynca operates locally within the user's browser.

Prompts and uploaded files are sent directly from the user's device to the selected AI platforms. 

User Device
↓
AISynca
↓
ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude / Grok

AISynca servers do not receive, process, store, or retain prompts or uploaded files.

Third-Party Services

  • ChatGPT
  • Gemini
  • Claude
  • Grok
  • Lemon Squeezy

Each service operates under its own terms and privacy policies.

Payments and Subscriptions

Subscription management and payment processing are handled by Lemon Squeezy.

AISynca does not store payment information or billing details.

Data Retention

License information and inquiry records are retained only as long as necessary for service operation and support.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as features and supported AI platforms evolve.
