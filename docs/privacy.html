Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

AISynca ("the extension", "we", "our") respects user privacy and minimizes data collection. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information when you use our Chrome Extension.

Information We Collect

AISynca only collects the minimum information required to operate the service.

Collected information:

  • License information required for subscription verification and feature access
  • Inquiry content voluntarily submitted by users via email support

AISynca does NOT collect, store, or receive:

  • AI account credentials or session cookies
  • Chat history or active responses from AI services
  • Saved memories from your AI platforms
  • Prompts typed into the dashboard
  • Uploaded files, spreadsheets, or images
  • Credit card, banking, or direct payment details

Payment processing is securely handled by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy, and payment information is not processed or stored by AISynca.

Prompt and File Handling

AISynca operates entirely locally within the user's browser. Prompts and uploaded files are sent directly from the user's device to the selected AI platforms' official endpoints. 

User Device
    ↓ (Local processing)
AISynca Chrome Extension
    ↓ (Direct browser requests)
ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude / Grok (Official API & Web Endpoints)

AISynca servers do not receive, process, store, or retain your prompts, text input, or uploaded files. All interactions are securely maintained within your browser session.

Third-Party Services

AISynca interacts with third-party services selected by the user:

  • ChatGPT (OpenAI)
  • Gemini (Google)
  • Claude (Anthropic)
  • Grok (xAI)
  • Lemon Squeezy (Payment & subscription provider)

Each platform operates under its own respective terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review their privacy standards before using their integrations.

Payments and Subscriptions

Subscription management, billing, and payment processing are securely handled by Lemon Squeezy. AISynca does not store payment information or billing details on our servers.

Data Retention

License keys and inquiry records are retained only as long as necessary for active service operation, validation of subscriptions, and support delivery.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as features, compliance rules, and supported AI platforms evolve. We will post any changes on this page.
